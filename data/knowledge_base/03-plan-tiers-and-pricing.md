# Plan Tiers & Pricing

Nimbus offers four plans. Prices are in USD and exclude applicable taxes/VAT.

| Plan | Price | Workflows | Team seats | Run history | Support |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Free** | $0 | 3 | 1 | 7 days | Community |
| **Starter** | $29 / month | 25 | 5 | 30 days | Email (48h) |
| **Pro** | $99 / month | Unlimited | 20 | 90 days | Priority email (8h) |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Unlimited | Unlimited | 1 year+ | Dedicated CSM + SLA |

Annual billing saves **two months** versus monthly (pay for 10, get 12).

## What's included by plan

- **Free** — evaluate Nimbus: 3 workflows, a single seat, community forum support.
- **Starter** — small teams: 25 workflows, 5 seats, email support, basic webhook
  and Slack integrations.
- **Pro** — growing teams: unlimited workflows, 20 seats, SSO, priority support,
  all integrations, higher API rate limits.
- **Enterprise** — custom contracts: unlimited everything, SAML SSO, audit logs,
  data residency choice, a dedicated Customer Success Manager, and a contractual
  uptime SLA. Enterprise pricing and custom terms are handled by our sales team.

## Upgrading & downgrading

- Upgrade anytime under **Settings → Billing → Plan**; the change is immediate and
  charges are prorated for the remainder of the current cycle.
- Downgrades take effect at the **end** of the current billing cycle. If you exceed
  the lower plan's limits (e.g. too many workflows), you must reduce usage first.

## Seats & overages

Adding a seat beyond your plan's included count is billed per additional seat at the
plan's per-seat rate, shown at checkout. Nimbus does not silently overage-charge —
you are prompted before any seat addition takes effect.
