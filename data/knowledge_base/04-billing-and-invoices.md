# Billing & Invoices

How Nimbus billing works, where to find invoices, and how to update payment details.

## Payment methods

- Nimbus bills through **Stripe**. We accept major credit and debit cards on all
  paid plans.
- **ACH bank transfer** and **invoice/PO billing** are available on Enterprise.
- Update your card under **Settings → Billing → Payment Method**. The change applies
  to your next charge.

## Billing cycle

- Paid plans renew automatically on the same calendar day each month (or each year
  for annual plans).
- A receipt is emailed to your account's billing contact after every successful
  charge.

## Finding your invoices

1. Open **Settings → Billing → Invoices**.
2. Every invoice is listed with its date, amount, and status.
3. Click **Download** to save a PDF, or **View** to see line items.

Invoices include your business name, address, and VAT/Tax ID if you've added them
under **Settings → Billing → Tax Details**.

## Failed payments

If a charge fails (expired card, insufficient funds):

1. We retry the charge up to **3 times over 7 days**.
2. You'll receive an email after each failed attempt.
3. Update your payment method to retry immediately.
4. If payment still fails after the retry window, the account is downgraded to the
   Free plan; your workflows are paused but **not deleted**.

## Disputes & incorrect charges

If you believe you were **charged incorrectly or double-charged**, do not dispute
with your bank first — contact Nimbus support with the invoice number. Billing
adjustments require account verification and are handled by a human agent. See also
*Refund Policy*.

## Tax & VAT

Customers in the EU, UK, and other VAT regions can add a valid VAT ID under **Tax
Details** to apply reverse-charge where eligible. Taxes are calculated at checkout
based on your billing address.
