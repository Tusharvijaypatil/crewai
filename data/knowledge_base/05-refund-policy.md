# Refund Policy

This policy explains when Nimbus issues refunds. For payment mechanics see
*Billing & Invoices*.

## Annual plans — 14-day money-back guarantee

New **annual** subscriptions are eligible for a **full refund within 14 days** of the
initial purchase, no questions asked. Request it from **Settings → Billing → Help**
or by contacting support with your invoice number. After 14 days, annual plans are
non-refundable but you keep access until the end of the paid year.

## Monthly plans

Monthly subscriptions are **not refundable**, but you can cancel anytime to stop
future charges. When you cancel:

- You keep paid features until the end of the current billing cycle.
- No partial-month or prorated refunds are issued for the unused portion.

## Cancellations

Cancel under **Settings → Billing → Cancel Plan**. Cancellation stops the next
renewal; it does not trigger an immediate refund. Your data is retained for 30 days
after downgrade in case you return.

## Exceptions handled by support

The following are reviewed case-by-case by a human agent and are **not** auto-approved:

- Duplicate or double charges (we refund verified duplicates in full).
- Billing errors caused by Nimbus.
- Extended service outages that breach an Enterprise SLA (SLA credits apply).

To start any of these, contact support with your invoice number and a description.
Refunds, when approved, are returned to the original payment method within 5–10
business days.
